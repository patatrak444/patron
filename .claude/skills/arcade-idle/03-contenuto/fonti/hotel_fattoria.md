# Gameplay content of My Perfect Hotel (com.master.hotelmaster) and Farm Land (com.loltap.farmland), as of October 2026

Scope: content only, not graphics. Research done 29 Oct 2026, about 24 tool calls. "Verified" means a primary source says it (store listing, publisher blog, or a hands-on review). "Estimated" means I inferred it. Several community sources (appgamer.com Q&A on hotel count, GamesBeat original) could not be fetched; see Gaps.

## Q1 — My Perfect Hotel: what layers does it add on the base loop, and how many hotels exist?

### Takeaway
MPH is the base arcade-idle loop with a hotel theme: you check guests in, clean rooms, restock toilet paper, and collect cash and tips. It then adds several layers on top: multiple themed hotels, each with its own currency; amenity stations; staff; room design choices; a Hotel Pass (battle pass); daily missions; seasonal events, such as Halloween Ghost Hunt (v1.39.0, Oct 2026) with a merchant, a prize wheel and themed guests; pets; and a standalone licensed location, Barbie Dream Hotel (23 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2027). No source I could reach gives an official hotel count. A 2023 community comment and a Q&A title suggest at least 8 hotels at that time.

### Inferences
- MPH's depth comes from layering, not a deeper production chain. Each hotel resets the same service loop under a new theme and its own currency. Around that sit meta systems: pass, events with a merchant and wheel, leaderboard, skins, pets and IP locations. These target long-term retention, which SayGames states openly.
- The Halloween event shows the template for events: one theme verb (vacuum), one event currency (pumpkins/candy), a shop, a wheel, and collectible themed guests.
- The hotel count is probably around 8–10 or more as of 2026, not counting event hotels. This is an estimate from a 2023 "8+" comment and does not meet the verified standard.

## Q2 — Farm Land: how do crops, animals, processing and selling chain together, and why is it weaker in content?

### Takeaway
Farm Land (Homa Games, Android/iOS since Apr 2021) has a short, mostly parallel chain. You sow, water and harvest field tiles, store the crop in a barn, carry it, and sell it at town merchants. Animals and trees (milk, wool, fruit) and fishing run through swipe/tap mini-games, and their output is sold directly. No source mentions a processing step (crop → product). Workers fill barns but don't sell. Growth comes from buying tiles across 4 maps, upgrading barns and workers, and gem helpers. Its content is thinner than MPH for several reasons: no processing tier, no customers with requests, and version notes that have read only "bug fix" for a long time. There are also no visible live events. Monetization leans on subscriptions and boosts.

### Cited Findings

**Identity and scale**
- Developer: Homa Games. Google Play: 100M+ downloads, 4.4 stars, about 778–842K reviews, updated 1 Oct 2026. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.loltap.farmland&hl=en)
- iOS: 4.8 stars from about 95K ratings. Latest version 3.28.13, dated "Oct 2". Every recent version (3.20.2 → 3.28.13) carries only the note "Bug fix and performance improvements." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)
- Released Apr 2021 on Android/iOS and Feb 2023 on the web. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/it/game/farm-land)
- Switch "Farm Land" (published by QubicGames, $4.99, 22 Sep 2023) is the same Homa game. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)

**C1 Core loop (verified)**
- Plant, water, harvest, sell to town merchants, then expand fields, barns and the island. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.loltap.farmland&hl=en); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)

**C2 Stations and chain**
- Crops named are wheat, tomatoes, apples and corn/maize. Animals named are cows (milk), sheep (wool, given as "cotton"), hens (eggs) and bees. There is also river and coastal fishing. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.loltap.farmland&hl=en); [LevelWinner, 13 Jul 2021](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- Each soil tile has 36 squares. Seeding and harvesting cover 3 squares per pass (two passes per plot). Watering covers 6 (one pass). — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- Mini-games: milking (alternating long swipes), tree shaking (left-right swipes), fishing (rapid taps until the spot is cleared), shearing (swipe over the wool). Their output is sold for money. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/); [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)
- Selling: a fruit and vegetable stall, plus a second market for eggs and milk that unlocks later. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/it/game/farm-land)
- Processing (for example wheat → bread or milk → cheese) is not mentioned in any source. CrazyGames explicitly lists none. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/it/game/farm-land)

**C3 Carried stack / storage**
- Barns start at a capacity of 100 per crop. When a barn is full, workers stop. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)
- The VIP subscription gives +50% barn capacity. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)
- A reader comment claims storage maxes at Lv100 and workers at Lv21 (unverified). — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- Player carry capacity: not found.

**C4 Unlocks and expansion**
- There are 4 maps or worlds, each with its own money stash. Tiles are bought with coins and some are gated by a tile count. The border tile unlocks the next map. Each section has 1 barn and 1 ship, and the ship gives fast travel. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- The first plot costs 7 coins (you start with exactly 7) and the next costs 20. Tiles are bought automatically by standing next to them. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)

**C5 Workers and automation**
- A barn offers 4 upgrades: 3 worker hires and 1 barn upgrade. Workers store crops but do not sell them. Upgrades speed up every step, including walking. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- The first worker costs 60 coins and tends corn. Workers are slow. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)
- Each world has helpers: one buys all crops and one empties all barns. Later helpers harvest trees and tend animals for gems. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)
- Special NPCs are online-only. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)

**C6 Customers**
- There are no customers with requests. Selling goes to fixed merchants/stalls. Sell prices: maize 3, tomato 4, apple 20 coins. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)

**C7 Economy and pacing**
- Coins plus gems. Gems come from unicorns that appear on the map. 53 costumes at 50 gems each (Switch version). — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)
- Golden watering can and golden scythe give 5 or 10 minutes of super mode (wider reach). Piggy banks, golden clovers, horseshoes and horns of plenty give coins, and ad rewards scale with progress. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/); [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- Unskippable ads appear during "rain". Videos run 10–30 s. Playing offline removes ads and rain. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- iOS IAP (USD): VIP weekly $5.99 (3-day trial), monthly $19.99, yearly $99.99 (bike, parrot pet, +50% barn, no ads); Free Rain 3.99; Starter Pack 2.99; Super Bundle 1.99; Break the Piggy Bank 2.99; Cat 3.99; Dog 4.99; Unlimited Power 14.99; Small Diamond Pack 1.99. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)

**C8 Long-term progression**
- 4 maps/worlds, with exploration "for new crops." — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)
- Live events, a pass, collections and leaderboards are not mentioned in any source. Update notes are generic. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)

**C9 Onboarding**
- The tutorial takes about 1 minute. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/)
- You start with 7 coins, which buys exactly the first plot. Corn grows in seconds and gives 30+ cobs. Each tool is one press and seeds are chosen automatically. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)

**C10 Theme expressed in mechanics**
- Farming verbs are mapped to gestures: walk-over tools for fields, swipes for milking, shaking and shearing, taps for fishing. "Rain" carries ads and is sold as an IAP ("Free Rain"). Island ships handle travel. — [LevelWinner](https://www.levelwinner.com/farm-land-farming-life-game-guide-tips-cheats-strategies/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162)
- There is no day/night cycle. — [LadiesGamers](https://ladiesgamers.com/farm-land-review/)

### Inferences
- The chain is wide but shallow. There are many raw sources (crops, trees, animals, fish), each going straight to a merchant, with no transformation tier and no customer demand. In MPH, guests create demand and pressure; in Farm Land, the player is the only bottleneck.
- Why it is weaker in content: (a) no processing; (b) no request/patience customers; (c) only 4 maps; (d) no visible live ops, with years of "bug fix" notes on iOS; (e) automation is partial, since workers fill the barn but someone must still sell. Monetization works on boosts and convenience (rain, VIP barn, power) rather than new content.
- The mini-games add variety but sit outside the stack loop.
